Questions

Do you repair iPhone and Android?

Yes. iPhone is our main work, and we also repair Samsung, OnePlus, Vivo, Oppo, Xiaomi and other Android phones. Send your model on WhatsApp to confirm parts.

How much does glass repair cost?

It depends on the model and whether only the glass or the full display is damaged. Send a photo on WhatsApp and we'll quote before you come in.

How long does it take?

Most front glass, back glass and battery jobs are done the same day when the part is in stock. If we need to order a part, we tell you the date up front.

Will I lose my data?

Glass and battery repairs don't erase your phone. Back up your photos and chats anyway, and we'll ask before any step that could reset it.

Do you replace iPhone back glass without changing the whole body?

Yes. We replace only the cracked rear glass on iPhone, then check the cameras and wireless charging before you pick it up.

Should I bring a water-damaged phone in quickly?

Yes. Switch it off, don't charge it, and bring it in as soon as you can. We inspect it and tell you honestly what can be saved before any repair.
